   dtype matches. DEFENSE AT DO step 5b/6: harmonize.py reads via
   `pq.read_table(...).to_pandas()` and any dtype-incompat would surface
   as a cast error at harmonize time.

   tuple list for 1989. If DO step 4's anchor-probe missed a non-anchor

   RESTATUS / PLDEL / BIRATTND / DMAGE / MRACE / CSEX / DBIRWT / DPLURAL —
