Mitigation: every year that HAS a target reconciled byte-exact/within-tol (198+53, 0 fail) and the mutation tests for those exact validators are green — strong but not exhaustive corroboration for the target-less interior years. Residual risk logged.
2. **Cesarean era-crosswalk boundary years (2003–2004).** The schema says 2003–2004 store DELMETH5-style codes at the DMETH_REC position; I applied the ≤2004 (codes 3|4) rule there, mirroring the v1 validator. The v1 cesarean targets are sparse (2005, 2022 sampled — both within tol); 2003–2004 cesarean has no NVSR target row, so a boundary-year miscode would not be reconciliation-caught. Mirrors the validated logic exactly, but unverified at those two specific years.
3. **Linked pre-2005 scoping is a deliberate exclusion, not a computation.** If a future reader expects 1983–2004 IMR in `linked_imr_by_year.csv`, they get a documented pointer instead of a number. This is intentional (D3, mirrors the canonical validator's own owned-surface scoping) but is a scope decision a reader could disagree with — recorded as a residual in DECISION_LOG D3 (re-openable).
4. **FMR denominator basis.** FMR uses natality-microdata resident live births (L6, parquet-derived) where the validated `fetal_mortality_rate` target used NVSR-published live births; the resulting <0.006% LB-series difference is within the 0.02 rate tolerance for every reconciled year (0 fail) and is documented in the README, but the FMR cells for target-less years (pre-1995, 2024) carry this basis difference un-anchored.
