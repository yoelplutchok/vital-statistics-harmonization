VERIFY confirms both patterns work; receipt records both runtimes (96s natality, 58s linked).

2. **DuckDB-vs-pyarrow parity may mask a dtype-coercion bug** at the cell-value level even when row counts match. E.g., DuckDB's WHERE could coerce `tabulation_flag` to bool differently from pyarrow. Defense: row-count parity at 3/3 products is necessary-but-not-sufficient evidence; future C8.12 mutation-test scaffolding will harden this by injecting known violations and verifying both engines catch them. Defense: filtered-denominator pattern is documented in the script; receipt records the values for future cross-check.
